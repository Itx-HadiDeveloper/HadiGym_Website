Our Story

Built On The Floor Of
Abdullah Mall

Abdullah Mall Gym began as a single row of dumbbells and a promise: give Qasimabad a fitness space that felt premium, not intimidating. Today it has grown into one of Hyderabad's most trusted training clubs, home to beginners taking their first step and bodybuilders chasing their next competition.

Every corner of the gym — from the equipment we import to the trainers we hire — is chosen with one question in mind: will this actually help our members transform?

M. Karrar Hussain

Founder & Owner

"I built the gym I always wished existed in my own neighbourhood — no shortcuts, real results."

Our Mission

To make world-class strength training and coaching accessible to every man, woman and beginner in Hyderabad — inside one premium, judgment-free space.

Our Vision

To become Sindh's most respected fitness brand, known for real transformations, honest coaching and a community that trains like family.
